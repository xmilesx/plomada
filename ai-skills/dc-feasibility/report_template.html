Max Buildable Calculation

RF-1 does not use FAR as primary density control. Instead, density is controlled by lot occupancy (building footprint as % of lot area) and height limits.

Calculation: Max Buildable = Lot Area × Max Lot Occupancy × Stories
For this lot: {{ lot_sf }} SF × 60% = {{ max_footprint_sf }} SF footprint × 3 stories = {{ max_buildable_sf }} above grade

FAR comparison: If RF-1 used FAR 1.0, this lot would only allow {{ lot_sf }} SF. The lot occupancy method allows more density ({{ max_buildable_sf }}) because you can build multiple stories within the allowed footprint.

Actual buildable requires architectural design accounting for setbacks, party walls, and structural constraints.

Fire Protection Requirements
⚠️ Verify with DC Fire & EMS

Sprinkler requirements depend on building classification, occupancy type, and scope of work. This summary is for preliminary planning only. Consult DC DOB and FEMS for project-specific requirements.

⚠️ IMPORTANT: IBC Occupancy Classifications ≠ DC Zoning

R-2 and R-3 in this section are IBC (International Building Code) occupancy classifications used to determine fire protection requirements. These are NOT the same as DC zoning designations (RF-1, RF-2, RF-3, etc.).

IBC Classifications (Building Code):
• IBC R-3 = 1-2 family dwellings (townhouses, duplexes, 2-unit flats)
• IBC R-2 = 3+ dwelling units (apartment buildings)

DC Zoning (Land Use):
• RF-1 = Residential Flat zone (allows 2 units MOR)
• RF-2, RF-3, RF-4 = Higher density residential flat zones

This property is zoned RF-1 (zoning). A 2-unit conversion would be classified as IBC R-3 (building code).

Sprinkler Requirement Triggers - Summary
When sprinklers may be required - ALL ITEMS SUBJECT TO DC DOB CONFIRMATION
  • 3+ dwelling units: Building classified as IBC R-2, sprinklers likely required [IBC § 903.2.8 - verify DC amendments]
  • New construction: IRC § R313.1 requires sprinklers in new 1-2 family (DC adopted)
  • Change of use to IBC R-2: Converting from R-3 to R-2 may trigger requirement [DC Existing Building Code Ch. 10]
  • Level 3 alteration (>50% work area): May trigger full code compliance [IEBC § 505.1]
  • Height >3 stories: May trigger additional fire protection requirements
Sprinkler May Not Be Required
Common scenarios - VERIFY ALL WITH DC DOB AND FEMS
  • 2-unit flat conversion in existing building (if maintaining IBC R-3 classification) [verify w/ DOB]
  • Interior renovation without change of occupancy [verify scope thresholds w/ DOB]
  • Additions to existing non-sprinklered 1-2 family (if under thresholds) [verify w/ DOB]
  • Basement underpinning (if no change in unit count or occupancy) [verify w/ DOB]

⚠️ These are general guidelines only. Actual requirements depend on scope of work, existing conditions, and DC DOB/FEMS interpretation. Even when not required, voluntary sprinkler installation may reduce insurance costs and improve resale value.
